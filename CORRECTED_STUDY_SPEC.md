# Frozen corrected study specification

This document is normative for the corrected manuscript run. Legacy scripts and
legacy outputs remain available only for provenance.

## Data partitions

- Pathogenic and Benign variants are the only labels used to fit sequence
  calibrators, the learned path-energy model, and the downstream classifier.
- Likely-pathogenic and Likely-benign variants are a locked end-to-end validation
  set. Their labels are not used by any upstream fitted component.
- VUS labels are unavailable. VUS assignments are exploratory model outputs, not
  validated clinical reclassifications.
- Five-fold outer predictions on the definitive set are generated by refitting
  the complete sequence-calibration, path-scorer, and classifier pipeline inside
  each fold. These predictions are used for probability calibration and decision
  thresholds.

## Graph routes

- Every candidate is a contiguous directed AlzKB path.
- Allowed form: start Gene, zero or one typed gene-gene edge, Disease,
  Phenotype.
- Up to 24 route prefixes are retained per start gene by deterministic,
  relation-stratified selection. Every phenotype connected to a retained disease
  is then expanded.
- BiologicalProcess and Pathway nodes do not connect onward to the phenotype
  layer in the retained AlzKB release. They are therefore used only as route-gene
  annotations and are never described as traversed path nodes.

## Learned energy and target

- Fourteen route, endpoint, pathogenicity, and confidence features are
  standardized using the definitive training paths.
- The energy is `U(path) = w^T standardized_features`.
- The weights and an AD-core logistic readout are optimized on definitive labels
  using binary cross-entropy, L2 regularization, and bounded L-BFGS.
- The exact path target is
  `P(path | H) proportional to exp(U(path) / theta(H))`, with the single schedule
  `theta(H) = 0.35 + 0.90 H`.

## Metropolis operator

- The state is one complete valid path.
- Proposals use a full-support mixture containing 90% learned independence
  distribution `softmax(U(path)/(2 theta(H)))` and 10% uniform mass.
- Acceptance uses the complete Hastings ratio,
  `min(1, exp((U(proposal)-U(current))/theta(H)) *
  q(current)/q(proposal))`, which satisfies detailed balance for the stated
  target.
- Chains initialize at the first deterministically sorted shortest path.
- The mixing-aware revision count is
  `50 + 10 ceil(log2(number of candidate paths)) + round(25 H)`.
- The reported exact distribution is the stationary target available by finite
  enumeration. Independent-chain final-state distributions are reported
  separately to demonstrate that the implemented revision process approximates
  that target.

## Required comparisons

- Uniform path distribution.
- Fixed-temperature learned model.
- Learned model without confidence-interaction features.
- Learned model with both fixed temperature and no confidence interactions.
- Learned model without route-mechanism features.
- Full uncertainty-conditioned learned model.

## Claims boundary

- Uncertainty broadening is partly induced by the explicit temperature schedule
  and must be interpreted with the fixed-temperature/no-confidence ablations.
- Gene specificity comes from valid gene-gene routes and annotations of genes on
  those routes, not from different raw phenotype endpoint sets.
- Per-gene rankings exclude genes represented by fewer than five variants from
  headline claims.
- ClinVar-held-out results are internal validation, not external clinical
  evidence.
- VUS calls are exploratory hypotheses and must not be described as clinical
  reclassification without orthogonal evidence.
